## 🎯 Downloads vs Active Users: What We Learned

### Validated Relationships

| Download to MAU Conversion | Service Example | Rate |
|----------------------------|-----------------|------|
| **Freemium with large free tier** | Boosteroid | 36-73% |
| **Premium/Controller required** | Amazon Luna | ~100% |
| **Multi-platform access** | PlayStation Remote Play | >100%* |

*Exceeds 100% because users access via web, console apps in addition to mobile app downloads

### Industry Benchmarks

**Typical App Conversion Rates**:
- **Freemium gaming apps**: 5-15% download → active user
- **Premium gaming apps**: 20-40% download → active user
- **Cloud gaming (observed)**: 36-100% download → active user

**Why cloud gaming has higher conversion**:
1. **Intentional downloads**: Users specifically seeking cloud gaming (not impulse)
2. **Subscription model**: Most services require payment, filters casual users
3. **Tech requirements**: Need good internet, filters non-committed users
4. **Alternative access**: Web browsers reduce reliance on app downloads

**Source**: [Mobile App Statistics 2024](https://www.mobiloud.com/blog/mobile-app-statistics/)

## 🚨 Data Limitations

### Why This Data is Incomplete

1. **No Disclosure Requirement**
   - Private companies don't have to publish download stats
   - Public companies (NVIDIA, Microsoft, Sony) report aggregate metrics only

2. **iOS Browser Access**
   - Xbox, GeForce Now accessible via browser = **zero app downloads counted**
   - True user base larger than Android downloads alone

3. **Web-Based Services**
   - Luna primarily web-based (controller app is separate)
   - Many users never download any app
   - Downloads severely undercount actual users

4. **Google Play Brackets**
   - "10M+" could be 10M or 49M (5x variance)
   - "1M+" could be 1M or 4.9M (5x variance)
   - Imprecise for market sizing

5. **Geographic Fragmentation**
   - APKPure, Aptoide, other stores (not counted in Google Play stats)
   - China uses separate app ecosystem (excluded from this analysis)
   - Samsung Galaxy Store, Huawei AppGallery (not tracked)

## 💡 Key Insights

### What Downloads Tell Us (and Don't)

✅ **Do Validate**:
- Boosteroid market position (1.1M downloads > Luna 500K ✓ matches our user estimates)
- Amazon Luna scale (500K downloads ≈ 400-500K users ✓ validates our estimate)
- Growth trends (Boosteroid 32K/month > Luna 20K/month ✓ validates relative growth)

❌ **Don't Validate**:
- Xbox Cloud Gaming absolute numbers (no cloud-specific download data)
- GeForce Now mobile users (no disclosed mobile download data)
- PlayStation Remote Play (multi-platform access inflates MAU vs downloads)
- iOS users for Xbox/GeForce Now (browser access = zero measured downloads)

### Bottom Line

**Download data validates our methodology where available** (Boosteroid, Luna) but **cannot comprehensively validate our 27M global estimate** because:
- Major services (Xbox, PS, GFN) represent 75% of market but have limited/no download disclosure
- iOS restrictions mean browser access goes unmeasured
- Multi-platform access means downloads undercount active users

**Confidence remains MEDIUM** - our estimate is **supported but not proven** by download data.
