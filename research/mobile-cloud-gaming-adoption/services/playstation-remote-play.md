## Key Insights

### PlayStation's Cloud Gaming Strategy
1. **Hardware-First**: PlayStation Portal as primary cloud gaming device
2. **Mobile-Secondary**: Mobile still supported but deprioritized
3. **Premium Tier**: Cloud streaming locked behind highest subscription tier
4. **Local + Cloud Hybrid**: Remote Play (local streaming) + Cloud Streaming (server-based)

### Why Portal Overtook Mobile
1. **Dedicated Device**: Better controls than touchscreen
2. **PlayStation Ecosystem**: Native PS5 DualSense features
3. **Marketing Push**: Heavy promotion as PS5 accessory
4. **User Experience**: More reliable than mobile remote play
5. **5G Not Required**: Works on home Wi-Fi primarily

### Mobile Remote Play Position
- **Still Significant**: 11-16M mobile users is substantial
- **Accessibility Tool**: Allows playing PS5 games without dedicated hardware
- **Geographic Reach**: Available in 170+ countries (broader than Portal)
- **Free Access**: No additional hardware purchase needed (unlike $200 Portal)

| Total Cloud/Remote Users | ~46-52M | ~6-7M | ~3-4M |
| Mobile MAU Estimate | 11-16M | 1.5-4M | 0.8-1.5M |

## Important Distinctions

### Remote Play ≠ Cloud Gaming
- **Remote Play**: Streams from YOUR PS5 to mobile device (local network or internet)
- **Cloud Gaming**: Streams from Sony's servers (PS Plus Premium only)

### What 36% Includes
The 36% figure includes:
1. Local Remote Play (streaming from own PS5)
2. Cloud Streaming (streaming from Sony servers via PS Plus Premium)
3. All devices (Portal, mobile, PC, PS4, PS5)

### True "Cloud Gaming" Subset
- **PS Plus Premium Subs**: ~7-9 million
- **Active Cloud Streamers**: Unknown, but 70% of Portal owners use it
- **Estimated Cloud Gaming Users**: ~5-7 million (subset of 46-52M total)
- **Mobile Cloud Gaming**: Much smaller than Remote Play
