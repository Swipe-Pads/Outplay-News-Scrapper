Honkai: Star Rail version 2.6 adds new character Fugue and endgame content

By James Rodriguez November 3, 2025

HoYoverse has announced version 2.6 for Honkai: Star Rail, introducing the new 5-star character Fugue alongside significant endgame content expansions. The update launches November 20th across all platforms.

Fugue is a Break Effect support character with the Fire element, designed to enhance teams focused on breaking enemy toughness bars. Her kit includes team-wide buffs and a unique mechanic that extends break damage durations. Early theorycrafting suggests strong synergy with existing Break-focused units like Ruan Mei and Firefly.

The update also introduces a new permanent endgame mode called Apocalyptic Shadow, joining the existing Memory of Chaos and Pure Fiction challenges. This mode emphasizes single-target damage and boss mechanics, rewarding Stellar Jades and exclusive light cones for completion.

Additional features include new story quests continuing the Xianzhou Luofu arc, quality of life improvements to the relic farming system, and a special login event offering free pulls. The version 2.6 banner will run Fugue alongside a rerun of Kafka's limited banner.

Honkai: Star Rail continues to perform strongly in mobile gaming revenue charts, consistently ranking in the top 10 grossing games globally since its April 2023 launch.
