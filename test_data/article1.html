Marvel Snap adds new Venom War season with Spider-Man variants

By Sarah Chen November 4, 2025

Marvel Snap has launched its latest season, Venom War, bringing a host of new Spider-Man themed card variants to the popular mobile card battler. The update introduces five new character cards including Scarlet Spider, Anti-Venom, and Agent Venom.

The season's spotlight cache features exclusive variant art for fan-favorite cards, with particularly striking designs for the new Symbiote-themed additions. Players can earn these through the season pass which runs through the end of November.

Second Dinner has also implemented several balance changes, adjusting power levels for cards like Galactus and She-Hulk to address community feedback. The studio says these changes aim to promote more diverse deck-building strategies.

New seasonal missions offer rewards including credits, boosters, and gold, while a special Venom-themed event runs for the first two weeks. The mobile game continues to see strong engagement with over 20 million downloads since launch.

Marvel Snap is available now on iOS and Android as a free-to-play title with optional in-app purchases. The Venom War season runs until November 30th, 2025.
