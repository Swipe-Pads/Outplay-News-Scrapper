Supercell reveals Clash Mini global shutdown date set for February 2026

By Tyler Mitchell November 2, 2025

Supercell has announced that Clash Mini, its auto-chess style strategy game, will officially shut down on February 28th, 2026. The game never progressed beyond its soft launch phase that began in November 2021.

In a statement to players, the Finnish mobile gaming giant explained that despite "passionate efforts" from the development team, the game didn't meet their standards for a global release. All in-app purchases have been disabled, and players who spent money will receive refunds.

Clash Mini featured miniature versions of characters from Clash of Clans and Clash Royale in tactical auto-battler gameplay. The game was available in select markets including Nordic countries, Spain, and several Asian regions but never reached broader availability.

This marks another project cancellation for Supercell, which is known for its rigorous "fail fast" approach to game development. The company previously shuttered games like Smash Land, Spooky Pop, and Battle Buddies during their development phases.

Existing Clash Mini players can continue playing until the February shutdown date. Supercell confirmed that the game's small but dedicated community will have access to all content free of charge during the final months.
