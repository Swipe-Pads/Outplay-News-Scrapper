| **Business Model** | D2C (Direct-to-Consumer) |

### What We Do
Displate creates and sells unique metal posters and wall art. Our products are:
- **Magnet-mounted** - no drilling required, easy to swap
- **Print-on-demand** - manufactured after order placement
- **Globally shipped** - customers worldwide
- **Licensed & original art** - from independent artists and major brands

### Key Differentiators
- Proprietary magnet mounting system
- Artist marketplace with revenue sharing
- Licensed content from major IP holders
- Eco-friendly: plant trees for every Displate sold
